    Frequently asked questions

    How much does custom software cost for a small business?

    In 2026, a focused internal tool or dashboard typically costs $8,000–$20,000 and takes two to three weeks. A client portal or workflow app with logins and permissions runs $15,000–$40,000. A customer-facing product lands between $25,000 and $75,000. AI-assisted development collapsed these numbers from the $100,000+ quotes that were standard five years ago.

    How accurate is this calculator?

    It produces a realistic range, not a quote. The ranges come from real projects and real market rates, and the biggest cost drivers — integrations, permissions, data migration, and AI features — are the inputs. A 30-minute scoping call turns the range into a fixed number.

    Why do custom software quotes vary so much?

    Because agencies price on their delivery model, not the problem. A traditional agency staffing a project manager, designers, and offshore developers for three months must charge $75,000+ to break even. A small senior team using AI-assisted development delivers the same tool in weeks for a fraction of that. Same software, different cost structure.

    Are there ongoing costs after the build?

    Yes: hosting (usually $20–$100 a month at small-business scale) and maintenance. Budget for a maintenance arrangement or an internal owner — custom tools die when nobody fixes breakages or adds the field the team asks for in month four.
